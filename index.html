08 — GOOD TO KNOW

Wondering about something?

Here's where the adventure begins. If you have another question, we're only a message away.

ASK US ON WHATSAPP ↗
Can you create a private safari?+

Yes. We can build a private itinerary around your group, preferred travel dates, accommodation style, pace and interests.

Can we combine multiple destinations?+

Absolutely. You can pair Maasai Mara with Amboseli, Tsavo, Samburu or other areas, or finish your safari by the Indian Ocean.

Are safari prices fixed?+

Every trip is quoted individually. Your price depends on the dates, group size, accommodation, route, transport and any extra activities.

When should we visit Kenya?+

Kenya offers wonderful experiences year-round. The ideal season depends on your priorities, including wildlife, migration timing, weather and the places you want to visit.

How do I start planning?+

Tell us your preferred dates, how many people are travelling and the experiences you have in mind. Use the form below, WhatsApp us or send an email.
