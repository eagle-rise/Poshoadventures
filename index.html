Travel guide

Useful before you pack your bags.

A simple planning framework for building a comfortable, memorable Kenya journey.

When to travel

Kenya works across different seasons, and the best timing depends on your priorities. Wildlife, landscapes, photography, coast time and budget can all change the ideal route.

What to pack

  • Light layers for changing temperatures
  • Comfortable closed shoes
  • Sun protection and reusable water bottle
  • Binoculars and camera equipment
  • Small day bag for game drives

How to plan

Choose your must-see places first, then decide how many nights you can give each. Avoid packing too many parks into too few days — the journey is part of the experience.

Photography

For photographers, we can structure days around light and time in the field. Bring spare batteries, memory cards, a dust-safe bag and the longest lens you are comfortable carrying.

Responsible travel

Respect wildlife space, follow park rules, support local communities and choose experiences that value conservation and the people who live alongside Kenya's landscapes.

Budget planning

Your final cost depends on transport, accommodation level, park fees, group size, trip length and activities. Ask for a tailored quote rather than assuming one package fits every traveler.
