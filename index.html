The Posho way

Wild places. Human stories. Real connection.

Posho Adventures is a Kenya-based tour company creating immersive journeys across the country's most extraordinary landscapes. Our trips are designed to balance exceptional game viewing with time to slow down, meet people, taste local life and experience Kenya beyond the usual route.

Whether you are dreaming of the Maasai Mara, Amboseli's elephant country, the dramatic north, the coast or a tailor-made multi-park safari, we build the journey around your interests, pace and budget.

No. The displayed USD prices are indicative “from” rates for planning. Your confirmed quote depends on travel dates, accommodation category, number of travellers, park fees, transport and activities.

Can you create a private safari?

Yes. Private trips can be arranged around your preferred pace, accommodation, destinations and interests.

Can we combine safari and beach?

Absolutely. A classic option is Maasai Mara followed by Diani, but we can build other combinations too.
